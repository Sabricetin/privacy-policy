Frequently Asked Questions

A creation failed. Will my credits be refunded?

Yes. If an image or video cannot be created, the credits used are refunded automatically, usually within a few minutes. If your credits are still missing after an hour, please contact us with the date and time of the attempt.

Does the app have a subscription?

No. DreamVision uses one-time credit packs purchased through Google Play. There is no automatic renewal, and credits do not expire.

My purchased credits are not showing in my account
  1. Close the app completely and reopen it.
  2. Open the credits screen and tap Restore Purchases.
  3. Make sure you are signed in with the same DreamVision account and the same Google account used for the purchase.

If the issue persists, please send us your Google Play order number. It begins with GPA. and can be found in your Google Play order history or in the purchase receipt email.

Can I request a refund for a purchase?

Payments are processed by Google Play. Within 48 hours of purchase, you can submit a refund request directly to Google Play: Google Play refund help.

After this period, or if the issue was caused by our service, please contact us with your order number and we will review your request.

How do I redeem a promo code?

Go to Settings → Promo Code, enter the code and tap Apply. Promotional credits are added to your account immediately.

Promo codes require a registered account and cannot be used in guest mode. Each code may be redeemed once per account, and codes are not case-sensitive.

What is the difference between guest mode and a registered account?

With a free account (email or Google), you can save your dreams to your gallery, access them from other devices and redeem promo codes. To create an account, go to Settings → Create Account.

Voice input does not convert my speech to text
  • Make sure microphone access is granted to DreamVision in your device settings.
  • Voice input uses your device's speech recognition service. Please ensure the Google app is installed and up to date.
  • If the issue persists, you can enter your dream as text.
The generated image does not reflect my dream

AI produces the best results from detailed descriptions covering the setting, people, colors, lighting and atmosphere. Brief or general descriptions provide the model with insufficient information. We recommend describing a single scene in detail or generating the image again.

I received a notice that my content was filtered

DreamVision uses automated safety filters to prevent the creation of sexually explicit, violent or harmful images. If your description triggers a filter, you may try describing the dream in a more symbolic or indirect way. Filter decisions are automated and cannot be overridden manually.

How can I report an inappropriate image or video?

Tap the flag icon at the top of the result screen or the dream detail screen, select a reason and submit the report. All reports are reviewed, and content that violates our rules is removed.

How can I delete my account?

You can delete your account in the app via Settings → Delete Account. The data that is deleted and retained is described in detail on the Delete Account page.
