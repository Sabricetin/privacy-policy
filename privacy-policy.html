Privacy Policy

How DreamVision collects, uses and protects your personal data.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Summary

  • Your dream descriptions are processed solely to create your images and videos.
  • The app does not display advertising, and your personal data is never sold.
  • Voice input is converted to text on the device; audio recordings are never transmitted to us.
  • You may delete your account and data at any time from within the app.

Data Controller

DreamVision is developed by Sabri ÇETİN, who is the data controller for your personal data. Contact: supportdreamvisionapp@gmail.com.

Information Collected

Account Information

  • If you create an account: your email address, display name and a user identifier. If you sign in with Google, your name and email address are shared with us by Google.
  • In guest mode, only an anonymous user identifier is created.

Dream Content

  • The dream text you type or dictate, the options you select (mood, time of day, point of view, style) and the images and videos created from them.
  • Dreams saved to your gallery and those marked as favorites.

Voice Data

  • During voice input, your device's speech recognition service (typically Google) converts your speech to text. Only the resulting text is transmitted to us; audio is never received or stored.

Credits and Purchases

  • Your credit balance and transaction history (credits added, used and refunded).
  • Purchase details: product, order number and date. Payments are processed by Google Play; we have no access to your card details.
  • Promo codes you redeem.

App Usage and Device Information

  • In-app interactions, such as screens viewed and features used (Google Analytics for Firebase).
  • Crash reports including device model, Android version and app version (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • A notification token and language preference, if you enable notifications.

Security and Abuse Prevention

  • An irreversible hash of your IP address, retained for 2 days to prevent repeated claiming of free credits. The IP address itself is not stored.
  • Content reports you submit: the reason, your optional note and a link to the reported image or video.

We do not collect your location, contacts or the photos stored on your device. When you save a creation to your device, it is only written to your gallery.

Purposes of Use

  • To create, display and save your images and videos.
  • To manage your account, credits and purchases, and to verify purchases through Google Play.
  • To send notifications you have requested, such as when your video is ready.
  • To prevent abuse and fraud, and to review content reports.
  • To resolve errors and improve the app.
  • To respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

The app does not display advertising, your personal data is never sold, and we do not send marketing emails.

AI Processing

To create an image or video, your dream text and selected options are sent to Google Gemini (via Google Cloud Vertex AI) to prepare an English prompt. The prompt is then sent to fal.ai, which generates the image or video. These providers process data solely for the purpose of providing this service. Your name and email address are never shared with them.

Automated safety filters may block descriptions or results that are sexually explicit, violent or harmful. AI-generated results may be unexpected; any inappropriate content can be reported from within the app.

Service Providers

Data is shared only with the following providers involved in delivering the DreamVision service:

ProviderPurpose
Google FirebaseAuthentication, database, file storage, server functions, notifications, analytics and crash reporting
Google Cloud Vertex AIConverting dream descriptions into prompts with Gemini
fal.aiImage and video generation
AdaptyCredit packs and purchase records
Google PlayPayments and purchase verification

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users and others. Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States.

Retention

Your account information and dreams are retained until you delete them or your account. Certain records are retained longer for legal and security reasons. Video progress records are deleted after 7 days, IP hashes after 2 days and crash reports after 90 days. Full details are available on the Delete Account page.

Your Rights

Depending on the legislation applicable to you (such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation or Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698), you have the right to access, rectify, erase or obtain a copy of your personal data, and to object to its processing. You may delete your account at any time via Settings → Delete Account. Other requests may be submitted to supportdreamvisionapp@gmail.com and will be answered within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

DreamVision is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with information, please contact us and the data will be deleted.

Data Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Access to data is restricted by security rules, and credit transactions are processed exclusively on our servers.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. Updates are reflected in the date at the top of this page, and material changes are also announced within the app.
