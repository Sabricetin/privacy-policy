Account Deletion

How to delete your DreamVision account and what happens to your data.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

App: DreamVision · Developer: Sabri ÇETİN

Deleting Your Account in the App

  1. Open DreamVision and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately, as described below.

Requesting Deletion by Email

If the app is no longer installed on your device, please send an email to supportdreamvisionapp@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject “Delete my DreamVision account”. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

In guest mode, dreams are not saved; the only record is an anonymous credit balance that is not associated with your identity.

Data That Is Deleted

  • Your account (email address, display name, sign-in)
  • All your dream images and videos, and the dream texts you wrote
  • Your credit balance and transaction history
  • Your purchase profile at our payment provider (Adapty) — automatically
  • Device keys used to send you notifications

Any remaining credits are forfeited upon account deletion and cannot be refunded or restored.

Data That Is Retained, and Retention Periods

  • Purchase records (order number, product, number of credits, date and your user ID): kept as long as required for tax and accounting obligations, refunds and fraud prevention.
  • Abuse-prevention record (whether starter credits were granted and how many free creations were used, without any content): kept so that free credits cannot be claimed repeatedly.
  • Promo code records (which codes your account used): retained to prevent a code from being redeemed more than once.
  • Video creation records (temporary records used to show progress while a video is being made; they include the dream text sent for the video): deleted automatically 7 days after creation. Your saved videos are not affected.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): deleted automatically by Google after 90 days.
